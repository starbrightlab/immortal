# Wake word models

`okay_nabu.tflite` and `hey_jarvis.tflite` (and their `.json` manifests) are the microWakeWord v2
models from [esphome/micro-wake-word-models](https://github.com/esphome/micro-wake-word-models)
(commit `40ff33f57f8f`), trained by Kevin Ahrendt with
[microWakeWord](https://github.com/kahrendt/microWakeWord). Licensed under the Apache License 2.0.
They are used unmodified.
